1. COMPUTATIONAL ENVIRONMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Due to severe firmware-level instability in the pre-release AMDGPU Linux driver 
stack (ROCm/Mesa Rusticl) for the RDNA 4 architecture (gfx1200) resulting in 
hardware TDR ring timeouts during highly divergent symbolic evaluations, the 
engine was transitioned to a Numba-accelerated Parallel CPU JIT. 

This JIT compilation reduced the execution time per generation (Population: 2048, 
N=1000) to an astonishing ~1.6 milliseconds (approx. 5.0 seconds for 3000 
generations), outperforming the OpenCL GPU implementation's overhead-bound 
execution speed (~12s) and completely resolving all hardware lockups.
