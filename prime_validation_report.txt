2. PHASE 11: THE ANTI-HALLUCINATION TEST (PRIME GAPS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Objective: Test PRIME's Minimum-Description-Length objective against a dataset 
with no known simple deterministic structure, to prove it resists hallucination.

Dataset: 10,000 Prime Number Gaps (normalized).
Target: Predict X[t] given the preceding 6 gaps.
Compute: 4,096 population size across 100,000 generations.

Results:

pattern in the prime gaps, PRIME completely abandoned the search space.

generated by PRIME would have been pure overfitting.
